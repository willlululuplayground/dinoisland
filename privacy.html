Dino Island: Kylo Math – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · English · 简体中文

English

Dino Island: Kylo Math is a dinosaur game that helps young children learn numbers and early math. In short: the app collects no data at all.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information or usage data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names or other personal details are asked for.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases; the app is free.
  • No internet connection is used. The app works fully offline.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Information stored only on your device

Game progress (dinosaurs, eggs, food, toys, math progress) and settings are saved only on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

The app is designed to be safe for young children. It contains no links out of the app, no social features, no chat and no way to contact other people. The parent area opens only after pressing and holding the small grey gear button for two seconds.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. For questions, please open an issue at github.com/willlululuplayground/dinoisland/issues.

简体中文

Dino Island: Kylo Math 是一款帮助小朋友学习数字和基础数学的恐龙游戏。简而言之：本 App 不收集任何数据。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。App 不收集、不在服务器上保存、不出售、不分享任何个人信息或使用数据。

  • 没有账号，不需要登录，也不会要求输入名字或其他个人信息。
  • 没有广告、统计分析、追踪，也没有任何第三方 SDK。
  • 没有 App 内购买，完全免费。
  • 不使用网络，可完全离线使用。
  • 不访问相机、麦克风、照片、通讯录或位置。

只保存在你设备上的信息

游戏进度（恐龙、蛋、食物、玩具、数学进度）和设置只保存在你的设备上，不会离开设备。删除 App 即会删除这些信息。

儿童

App 的设计对小朋友是安全的：没有跳出 App 的链接，没有社交功能，没有聊天，无法联系他人。家长区需要按住灰色的齿轮小按钮两秒才能打开。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在本页面更新。如有疑问，请在 github.com/willlululuplayground/dinoisland/issues 提交问题。
