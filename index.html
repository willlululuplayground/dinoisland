Dino Island: Kylo Math

Hatch, raise and race dinosaurs. Learn numbers on the way.

A dinosaur game for young children (about 3–6 years old). Children hatch eggs, feed, wash and care for their dinosaurs, explore parks around the world and play friendly dinosaur sumo. Short math games open eggs, pay at the food shop and win new toys: counting, comparing, adding and taking away, number order and more, following the Kylo Math 1 lessons. Everything is spoken aloud, so children who cannot read yet can play.

Support

Parent area

Press and hold the small grey gear (top right of the main screen) for two seconds to open the parent area. There you can choose which math questions are used: the Grade 1 book in order, mixed Grade 1 games, or easy games for ages 2–4.
